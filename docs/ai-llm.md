# AI & LLM's

The biggest hype train since the dot com bubble! But this stuff is pretty cool and you can do some fun things with it.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a transformative technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks, solve problems, and make decisions that traditionally require human intelligence.

AI includes solutions that can process and analyze data, automate repetitive tasks, and forecast potentials based on looking at historical patterns.

LLMs are the the artificial intelligence systems designed to interpret the requests and generate the responses that form the core AI workload. LLMs are trained on large amounts of data - allowing the models to learn patterns, relationships between words, and other context. LLMs are not the same as the tools or solutions themselves. For example ChatGPT is using the GPT LLM - ChatGPT is not an LLM itself.
