# About

This site contains a variety of code snippets, explanations and resources that I have found helpful over the years. I've started each section with a brief overview of the technology / language but after this point there isn't a great deal of structure to the code snippets. This is not meant as a comprehensive guide to any one technology but more of a prompt on things you’re likely to come across when working in the world of development.

Why do this? The motivation comes from two places. One is somewhat altruistic in that I hope others may find useful information here (although there are certainly much better resources than this out there to learn from) but the main reason is that I'm bad at remembering stuff. I have a sporadic mess of word documents doted about (some more useful than others) and doing this allows me to clean house, refresh my knowledge and have it all in one neat place.

I’m a big fan of documenting things properly and sharing knowledge so this a nice opportunity to do both!

::: tip
A lot of the time I don’t really know what I’m talking about so this content comes with a caveat - I am not an authority on any topic, do your own research to supplement your learning and feel free to correct me on anything I’ve gotten wrong. The notes and examples I'm sharing are just bits and pieces that have helped me out while tinkering with different technologies and languages.
:::
